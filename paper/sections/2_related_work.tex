\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Mesh- and point-cloud-to-CAD.}
Learned methods map geometry to construction sequences: DeepCAD \citep{wu2021deepcad} and Point2Cyl \citep{uy2022point2cyl} predict sketch-and-extrude structure, and CAD-Recode \citep{rukhovich2025cadrecode} decodes point clouds into executable CadQuery code with a small LLM. CADFit \citep{nehme2026cadfit} instead optimizes: it slices the mesh into sketches, fits extrusion, revolve, fillet and chamfer candidates, and greedily composes them to maximize volumetric IoU, which lowers the invalid ratio on complex parts. Datasets include ABC \citep{koch2019abc}, Fusion 360 Gallery \citep{willis2021fusion360} and DeepCAD; CADBench \citep{doris2026cadbench} unifies them with face-count tiers and metrics for fidelity, executability and compactness. We do not propose a new reconstructor. We audit what an optimizer's reported score measures once its final program is replayed, and which search extensions survive that replay.

\paragraph{LLM agents with CAD tools.}
Tool-using language models \citep{yao2023react,schick2023toolformer} and agent--computer interfaces \citep{yang2024sweagent} motivate CAD agents. CAD-Assistant \citep{mallis2025cadassistant} plans FreeCAD actions with CAD-specific tools such as a sketch parameterizer and a cross-section generator. CADFather \citep{savrasov2026cadfather} coordinates learned and algorithmic proposal tools with a vision-language assistant, executes the proposed programs and keeps the best valid result. Our tools are the stages of one optimizer, and our controls are chosen to separate the tools from the model: the same agents without the tools, with only one tool, and two scripted policies that use the tools without an LLM.
