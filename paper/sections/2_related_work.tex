\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Mesh- and point-cloud-to-CAD.}
Learned methods map geometry to construction sequences: DeepCAD \citep{wu2021deepcad} and Point2Cyl \citep{uy2022point2cyl} predict sketch-and-extrude structure, zone graphs \citep{xu2021zonegraphs} search over extrusion sequences, and CAD-Recode \citep{rukhovich2025cadrecode} decodes point clouds into CadQuery code that is executed for evaluation. CADFit \citep{nehme2026cadfit} optimizes instead: it fits extrusion and revolve candidates to slices of the mesh and composes them greedily to maximize volumetric IoU. Datasets include ABC \citep{koch2019abc}, Fusion 360 Gallery \citep{willis2021fusion360} and DeepCAD; CADBench \citep{doris2026cadbench} unifies them with face-count tiers and metrics for fidelity, executability and compactness. We do not propose a new reconstructor; we measure an optimizer by its executed program and test which repairs survive that measurement on held-out parts.

\paragraph{Iterative and agentic CAD generation.}
Tool-using language models \citep{yao2023react,schick2023toolformer} and agent--computer interfaces \citep{yang2024sweagent} motivate CAD agents that act, observe and revise. CADReasoner \citep{kabisov2026cadreasoner} trains a model to edit a runnable CadQuery program from the geometric discrepancy of its previous output, and IterCAD \citep{hu2026itercad} trains a multimodal agent against an executable CAD sandbox. CAD-Assistant \citep{mallis2025cadassistant} plans FreeCAD actions with CAD-specific tools, and CADFather \citep{savrasov2026cadfather} coordinates learned and algorithmic proposal tools with a vision-language assistant, executes proposed programs and keeps the best valid one. We use frozen closed models without training, take the tools from one optimizer, and compare tool packages that differ only in implementation and checking policy on a cohort drawn after the packages were frozen.
