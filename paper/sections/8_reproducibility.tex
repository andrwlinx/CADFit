\section*{Reproducibility statement}
Every number in this paper is generated from committed result files by \texttt{bench/paper\_numbers.py}, and every figure by a committed script. The protocols, with their dated amendments and execution logs, the frozen source trees of every arm, the cohort definitions (seeded draws with source-model exclusion), the scoring and analysis code, and the per-part and per-episode records will be released with the paper. Closed-model episodes require an Anthropic API key; their raw records are included, so the analyses can be re-run without the API.
