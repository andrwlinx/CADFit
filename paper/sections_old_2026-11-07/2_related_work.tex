\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Mesh- and point-cloud-to-CAD.}
Learned methods map geometry to construction sequences: DeepCAD \citep{wu2021deepcad} and Point2Cyl \citep{uy2022point2cyl} predict sketch-and-extrude structure, zone graphs \citep{xu2021zonegraphs} search over extrusion sequences, and CAD-Recode \citep{rukhovich2025cadrecode} decodes point clouds into CadQuery code that is executed for evaluation. CADFit \citep{nehme2026cadfit} optimizes instead: it fits extrusion and revolve candidates to slices of the mesh, composes them greedily to maximize volumetric IoU, then recovers fillets and chamfers, and it reports lower invalid ratios than learned baselines on complex parts. Datasets include ABC \citep{koch2019abc}, Fusion 360 Gallery \citep{willis2021fusion360} and DeepCAD; CADBench \citep{doris2026cadbench} unifies them with face-count tiers and metrics for fidelity, executability and compactness. Our contribution here is an audit: how an optimizer's search score relates to its replayed final program, and which search extensions survive the replay.

\paragraph{Iterative and agentic CAD generation.}
Tool-using language models \citep{yao2023react,schick2023toolformer} and agent--computer interfaces \citep{yang2024sweagent} motivate CAD agents that act, observe and revise. CADReasoner \citep{kabisov2026cadreasoner} trains a model to edit a runnable CadQuery program from the geometric discrepancy of its previous output, and IterCAD \citep{hu2026itercad} trains a multimodal agent against an executable CAD sandbox. CAD-Assistant \citep{mallis2025cadassistant} plans FreeCAD actions with CAD-specific tools such as a sketch parameterizer and a cross-section generator. CADFather \citep{savrasov2026cadfather} coordinates learned and algorithmic proposal tools with a vision-language assistant, executes the proposed programs and keeps the best valid result. We use frozen open-weight models, take the tools from one optimizer, and add controls aimed at the tool-versus-policy question: the same agents without the tools, one model with a single tool, and two scripted policies that use the tools without an LLM.
