## A. Numbers and statistics

1. **Traceability.** Every experimental number in the manuscript (main text, tables, figures, captions, appendices) is produced by a script from a named source: results/fresh_arms.json, results/agent_skills.json, results/agent_extra.json, results/refine_study.json, bench/runs/fresh_scores.json and bench/runs/fresh_section_control.json (per-part figure inputs), or the defect-case records in B11. Result numbers in sections/*.tex are LaTeX macros from paper/numbers.tex, generated by bench/paper_numbers.py; configuration values (budgets, thresholds, resample counts) are traced to the configuration files/scripts that set them. Check: `python3 bench/paper_stats.py && python3 bench/paper_numbers.py` regenerates numbers.tex byte-identically, and a reader can map each table cell / figure value to a source field.
2. **Rounding.** Means and differences are reported to 3 decimals; an interval endpoint whose 3-decimal rounding would change its sign or make it 0.000 is printed to 4 significant digits (e.g. the Gemma−policy lower bound −0.0001087).
3. **Statistical specification.** The Setting section states: paired percentile bootstrap over parts, 10,000 resamples, seed 0; differences paired by part; Bonferroni applies only to the six-model family (α = 0.05/6) for skills-vs-code, agent-vs-pipeline and agent-vs-policy comparisons; search-arm comparisons are labelled exploratory nominal 95%; intervals reflect part sampling only, not episode randomness. If win/loss counts appear, their tie threshold is stated and they are described as counts, not as the bootstrap's hypothesis.
4. **Chamfer distance.** CD is reported as a mean over parts with a measured output, with its denominator (58/59/59/59 for A–D), alongside separate failure counts; no CD mean includes failures as 0.

## B. Claims on search (Q1, Q2)

5. **Gap per arm.** The paper reports mesh IoU − IoU(CAD) for A–D (−0.001, 0.046, 0.081, 0.049 to 3 decimals) and does not state a monotone relationship with search strength.
6. **Gap decomposition.** For arm C, the paper distinguishes 6 unbuilt and 3 built-but-OCC-invalid programs, and reports the signed contributions to the mean gap normalised over all 60 parts (0.078 from unbuilt, 0.003 from built) without claiming every built program matches its mesh (a reader can find the counterexample magnitude, e.g. the largest built-program discrepancy, in an appendix table or sentence).
7. **Gain shrinkage test.** The main results report paired (CAD gain − mesh gain) with intervals for B−A, C−B and D−A; B−A and D−A intervals exclude 0, C−B is called inconclusive; the text does not infer a difference between gains from significance on one metric and not the other.
8. **D vs A.** Main text reports D−A on IoU(CAD) (+0.067 [−0.012, +0.144]) and does not claim D improves executed fidelity over A overall.
9. **Section proposals.** Main text reports: D−C on IoU(CAD) with interval; the standalone best-axis section extrusion as a descriptive mesh-IoU number (0.842) with no CAD claim; D − best-of(C, sections) on IoU(CAD) with interval; the best-of selection rule (per part, the higher of C's model and the best section extrusion by IoU against the target mesh); per-split D−A estimates with intervals, without claiming a statistically established split interaction. Any "largest" wording is restricted to observed adjacent increments among A–D. No contribution is attributed to an individual rule.
10. **Measurement description.** The Setting section identifies the corrected base (A), the configuration of each arm (flags), the mesh judge (exact booleans, else GWN Monte Carlo), CAD replay (fresh sandboxed process, OCC validity of shape and every solid), the definitions unbuilt ⇒ IoU(CAD)=0; IoU(CAD) retains the measured scores of built-but-OCC-invalid solids; validity-gated = 0 unless built and valid.
11. **Defect evidence.** Each defect named in the paper has, in the appendix, an identified development/smoke case, the affected code path or configuration, and a recorded before/after score or failure behaviour; the text separates these case studies from fresh-cohort aggregates and does not attribute aggregate gains to individual fixes.
12. **Validity columns.** Table 1 and Table 2 include a validity-gated column; cells where validity was not recorded (best-of(C, sections)) read "n/m" with a footnote saying validity was not measured for that control.

## C. Claims on agents (Q3)

13. **Protocol.** Section 5 states: toolsets (code, code+section_extrude, skills) and their tool lists, prompt differences, from-scratch initialisation, 40-call / 30-min / 16k-token-per-turn limits, target-score access per toolset, best-observed-answer selection by IoU against the target mesh, failures scored 0; and that results assume target-score feedback (the target is the method's input). It discloses skill–pipeline differences: even-odd loop nesting, 1e-6 profile-area filter, revolve-split fix.
14. **Skills vs code.** All six skills−code differences appear with Bonferroni intervals; "improves all six" is stated only because all six intervals exclude 0.
15. **Scripted policies.** Both policies are described operationally: unrestricted (greedy best-improvement assembly; its free GT trials per part reported, mean ~69) and budget-matched (every GT-scored trial charged as one of the 40 calls; first-improving trial; 20 calls reserved for assembly; candidate order by IoU, adds then cuts; any remaining uncharged evaluations reported accurately). Agent-vs-policy differences (each model, skills) are reported with Bonferroni intervals against both; LLM benefit over a script is claimed only for a model whose agent-minus-budget-matched-policy Bonferroni lower bound exceeds 0, and only for that policy and setup; otherwise the text says the benefit is not established. A null result is not turned into "skills rather than planning explain the gain".
16. **Repeats and ablation (pending prerequisites).** Before acceptance, results/agent_extra.json covers all 20 parts × 2 extra Gemma skills runs, 20 Gemma code+section runs and 20 budget-matched policy runs (missing episodes scored 0 and counted). The paper reports Gemma skills as three separately listed 20-part run means plus their mean and min–max range, with the original run as the declared primary result; it does not treat 60 episodes as 60 parts nor the range as a confidence interval. Code+section is compared to both Gemma code and Gemma skills with intervals; tool-attribution statements are restricted to Gemma.
17. **No unsupported comparative claims (human check).** A reader finds no claim of equivalence, non-inferiority or superiority of agents vs the pipeline, of D vs A on executed fidelity, or causal attribution of gains to skills vs planning, in any wording; the Gemma–pipeline comparison is given as an observed difference with its interval.
18. **Refinement study.** Appendix only; described as descriptive mesh IoU on 19 gated parts, 2 repeats, with no-op and equal-time-search controls and W/L at ±0.01; no executed-CAD or validity claims.
19. **Qwen3.8 failure mode.** Main text says "16/20 code episodes terminated on a turn without a tool call". The thinking-exhaustion explanation appears only if supported by trace evidence cited in the appendix (e.g. finish_reason = length on the terminating turn); the 32k numbers are post-hoc observations, kept out of Table 2 and the abstract.

## D. Framing, figures, venue

20. **Scope qualifiers.** Title or abstract names mesh-to-CAD and the audited optimizer family; the abstract names hard-tier CADBench benchA/benchE and part counts (60, 20). The paper says the fresh cohort was drawn after, and disjoint from, all development sets (not that it is provably never inspected beyond that).
21. **Post-hoc labelling.** The section-proposal recipe's post-hoc origin and its freezing before the fresh cohort is drawn are both stated.
22. **Positioning (human check).** Related work names CADFit, CAD-Recode, CAD-Assistant and CADFather; each comparison cell (input, tools, final-program execution in evaluation, policy control) is supported by the cited paper; no "no prior work"-type claim.
23. **Limitations.** Names at least: one episode per cell except Gemma; 20-part agent sample; revolve tool rarely exercised; agent revolve fix absent from pipeline arms; single optimizer family audited; target-score feedback assumed.
24. **Figure 1.** Panel (a) labelled 60 parts: arms A–D and best-of, mesh vs IoU(CAD), error bars labelled as 95% bootstrap CIs of the mean computed from per-part data, unbuilt/invalid counts annotated for A–D, best-of validity shown as n/m (not measured). Panel (b) labelled 20 parts: code→skills per model, Gemma code+section and Gemma repeat range (labelled as a range, not a CI), pipeline D on the same 20 parts (0.904), both scripted policies with the unrestricted one labelled with its free-trial count. Zero-scoring failures are labelled as such. Every figure is referenced in the text.
25. **Venue.** Main body ≤ 9 pages in the ICLR 2027 style; anonymous; zero undefined citations/references.
26. **Reproducibility.** The statement lists the regeneration commands (bench/paper_stats.py, bench/paper_numbers.py, paper/figures/make_figures.py, bench/agentsk_score.py), the code revision, model/serving settings (bench/agentsk_models.py), and the cohort manifests (bench/cadbench/index.json, fresh/ file list, study_parts seed); running the three local commands reproduces numbers.tex and the figures.
