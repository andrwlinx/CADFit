# Paper acceptance contract — Execute the Program (2026-11-07)

status: accepted (round 2, 2026-11-07; reviewer thread 01a1b8ee-f3e7-7b62-88ec-21b1939da59d). FROZEN.

Checked against paper/main.pdf, its LaTeX source, and the permitted sources. Permitted sources for
empirical values: results/heldout/*.json (incl. pipe/ and pre-recovery merges),
results/heldout_pre_recovery/, results/tools_v3/*.json (incl. episodes/ and h2_timing.json),
results/closed/*.json, results/review_bundle_2026-11-05/ (episode records, diagnosis.json),
results/tools/profile_v2_local.json, and the execution logs in review-stage/HELDOUT_PROTOCOL.md and
review-stage/TOOLS_V3_PROTOCOL.md. Configuration constants and dates may cite protocols or code.

A1  Traceability. Every empirical value in the abstract, text, tables, captions, figures and
    appendix comes from \input{numbers.tex} or a figure script, both generated by committed code
    (bench/paper_numbers.py; paper/figures/make_*.py) from permitted sources; each macro's source
    field and calculation is documented in that code; rounding is reproducible (3 decimals for IoU
    and differences unless stated).
A2  Evaluation method (C1). The paper defines: execution of the delivered program in a sandbox;
    OCC validity of the shape and of every solid; the robust judge (exact booleans when both meshes
    are closed and consistent, else seeded Monte Carlo with winding numbers); the replay limit
    (900 s); validity gating (failed/invalid/missing delivery scores 0, every cohort part kept in
    the denominator); and each pipeline arm's intervention, including d0's infrastructure/seeding
    patches. Single-run arms (d0, dx) are distinguished from three-seed arms (v2, v3, trim); for
    three-seed arms the paper says whether validity counts are per run or "valid in every seed".
A3  Statistics. The paper states: paired per-part differences; repeats averaged within part;
    split-stratified bootstrap 95% intervals (nominal); exact sign tests; the better/worse
    thresholds (±0.005 for the held-out analysis, ±1e−9 for the agent analyses) with neutral
    counts; correction families (held-out primary alone; held-out secondary Holm over 4 declared
    tests; tools-v3 Holm over the 3 Opus tests; Sonnet tests uncorrected; Study 2 comparisons
    exploratory with nominal intervals).
A4  Held-out primary. dx − d0 is reported with estimate, interval, better/worse/neutral counts and
    p, labelled the preregistered primary, analysed alone (no multiplicity adjustment).
A5  Held-out secondary. All 4 declared secondary contrasts appear (v2 − dx, v3 − v2, trim − v3,
    trim − section-only); while trim − section-only is unavailable it is a visible placeholder and
    the displayed adjusted p-values are labelled "Holm over the 3 available tests, provisional";
    split-level (benchA/benchE) results are given for every arm.
A6  Failure classification. Invalid deliveries are decomposed by outcome: for d0, 23 built-but-
    invalid, 8 build errors/missing, 10 resource-limit replay failures (41/300 described as "no
    valid replay under the protocol", not as defective geometry); the same decomposition is given
    for every held-out arm and every agent condition, distinguishing valid deliveries, built-invalid
    deliveries, build errors, missing output, and resource/setup replay failures wherever present
    (d0: 259/23/7/1/10; an explicitly labelled combined build-error/missing count is acceptable),
    computed from episode or part records.
A7  Recovery amendment. Main text: post-outcome measurement amendment; eligibility classes
    (setup, resource), one terminal pass, unchanged programs, 531 affected records (299 dx setup,
    222 v2-seed-2 timeouts, 10 d0 resource). Original vs recovered values are given for every
    affected arm and every contrast involving it (at least dx − d0: −0.795 vs +0.104; v2 3-seed mean
    0.699 vs 0.930), with the cause.
A8  Trim. Per seed, over all 300 parts: gated_no_trim − v3, gated − gated_no_trim, and their sum;
    the first term is described as replay/repair effects including the frozen seed-0 timeout
    (00403399), not as pure repair benefit.
A9  Study 2 scope and validity. The closed-model comparisons are labelled exploratory; the paper
    says Opus+code exceeds optimizer v3 on the reserved 40 (nominal interval excludes 0) and that
    superiority over the trim package is not established; no sentence claims equivalence from a
    non-significant difference. The reserved 40 are identified as a subset of fresh2. The
    472/472 delivered-valid count is scoped to Study 2, and the 22 valid deliveries not verified
    in-episode are disclosed.
A10 Code toolset. "Code" is described as including target inspection (describe_target, section)
    and execution feedback (evaluate and executed checks); not an unaided LLM baseline.
A11 Closed-model settings: model ids, adaptive thinking, effort high, 32k max tokens per turn, full
    native history, budget 40 calls / 30 min, 3 episodes per part, offline re-judging.
A12 Tools-v3 reporting. Primary (Opus CADFit tools v3 − v2) reported with estimate and interval,
    stating that the declared sign test does not establish improvement (p 0.121; Holm 0.243) and
    not inferring equivalence or no effect. All six quality comparisons appear with testing status.
    The Opus CADFit-tools-vs-code advantage (Holm 0.028) and the descriptive timing contrasts
    appear; runtime summaries were prespecified, while the timing contrasts, intervals and sign tests
    are reported as descriptive analyses outside the declared quality-testing family.
A13 Tools-v3 causal scope. Tools v3 is described as a package of exact implementation changes plus
    a behavioural 120 s check cap; the paper does not attribute effects to either component alone
    and does not claim tools help "only" after overhead removal; output-equivalence claims are
    bounded to the development inputs checked (300 random cases; 162 dev sketches; 200 rotation
    cases; 18 judge pairs).
A14 Runtime. Pipeline: mean and median successful-attempt minutes per part per arm with
    denominators (299 measured per run), noting retries are excluded and parts normally ran three
    per node; full trim-package latency is stated as unavailable/partial. Agents: mean minutes per
    episode and model/tool share; nodes ran 3–8 episodes concurrently.
A15 Exclusions and reruns. Listed with cause and count: pipeline resubmissions/retries per the
    held-out protocol (incl. the node2801 chunks), the cancelled dx recovery job, node4002 Opus run
    (44 diagnosed episodes, 35 with records, $8.95 recorded), tools-v3 API-error episodes (239, moved,
    rerun), 53 preflight-blocked unstarted episodes, one ReadTimeout crash (no record, rerun).
    Spend is reported as recorded subtotals with coverage counts; unrecorded amounts are stated as
    unknown.
A16 Cohorts. fresh2 drawn after development; the reserved 40 drawn from fresh2's manifest before any
    fresh2 outcome; fresh3 drawn after the tools-v3 code and protocol froze, excluding every source
    model in any earlier set; the paper states fresh2's other 260 parts' pipeline outcomes were seen
    before the agent studies.
A17 Pending outputs. Unavailable outputs (section-only control, independent rejudge, surface
    agreement) appear only as visible placeholders; no claim about surface detail or independent
    confirmation is made from them. If any becomes available before submission it is incorporated
    and dependent calculations are updated.
A18 Limitations name at least: post-outcome amendment; 40-part agent cohorts; single benchmark
    (CADBench hard tier, two splits); IoU-centric endpoint with surface and rejudge pending; code
    baseline includes inspection tools; dependence on specific closed-model versions.
A19 Figures. Figures 1, 3, 4 exist; every plotted mean of a quality metric has a 95% interval; plotted
    runtime/cost means are labelled as means without intervals; captions state cohort, n, repeats;
    Figure 2 is labelled as a development-part example.
A20 Format. Main text ≤ 9 pages in the ICLR 2027 style; anonymous; compiles with no undefined
    references or citations; every \cite key resolves to a references.bib entry that existed before
    this rewrite or was added from DBLP/CrossRef; no "[VERIFY]" in the PDF.
