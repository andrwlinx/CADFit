## Synthesis

No new paper since 2026-10-19 pre-empts the remaining claims. CADFather (2610.09127) is still the closest competitor:
- It also does execution-aware selection.
- It counts invalid programs as score 0 and keeps the best valid one ("protected best").
- It has no repair step and does not use OCC validity checks.

Adoptable ideas, ranked by expected gain on our failure modes:
1. **A protected valid incumbent from the start** (CADFather's protected best, also the round-1 reviewer's item 6).
   - On dev4 almost all of the gated gain comes from avoiding zeros. The remaining zeros are no-model outcomes: 00247424 (OOM), and 00730725, 00966713 and 00814797 in some runs.
   - Fix: execute a cheap valid fallback early and deliver it if the main search fails or is killed.
2. **Memory limits on candidate subprocesses** so one runaway candidate cannot kill the part (00247424 is OOM in every arm).
3. **Post-execution critique for agents** (RA-CAD). This is a harness change; evaluate it later under matched budgets.
4. **SDF-based parameter polishing** (CADFather optimizer). Their own ablation shows only −0.004 to −0.006, so it is low priority.
